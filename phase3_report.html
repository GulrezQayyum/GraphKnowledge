q15: How are death and transformation related in Marcus's view?

Method: HYBRID | Passages: 10

Generated Answer:

Marcus Aurelius sees death as a natural transformation that merely dissolves the elements that had been combined into a living body, a process that is “the same mystery as generation” and therefore unremarkable or shame‑free for a rational being [bookIV_5]. He stresses that death is not an abrupt end but a continuing dissolution of matter, and that the soul, once freed from bodily corruption, simply “dissolves” into the elements, a process that is part of nature’s orderly cycle [bookII_12, bookII_17]. Thus, death is viewed not as a calamity but as the inevitable, natural transformation of the self back into the universal substance.
